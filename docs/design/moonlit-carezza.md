# Moonlit Carezza environment

The user selected the second environment concept on October 12, 2026, then requested distinctive Dolomite rock colors rather than generic gray mountains. The selected reference is [moonlit-carezza-reference.png](moonlit-carezza-reference.png). It is generated concept art, not a captured build.

## Implemented composition

`ForestPerimeterScenery` replaces the side/rear triangular mountain meshes and spherical shrubs with a world-anchored textured ridge, an irregular elliptical lake behind the forest entrance, a gravel/moss shore, clustered spruce trees and reused three-dimensional rocks. The forward teaching beds, routes, model evaluation, complete ensemble and manual/prepared-profile experiences remain intact.

The rock texture uses pale cream limestone, restrained rose highlights and cool blue-violet shadows. The existing winter sky and moon remain responsible for the sky. Transparent regions of the mountain texture reveal that sky. Distant terrain uses a curved mesh; its central 120 degrees preserve the texture's approximate proportions, while the outer wooded portions extend along the sides and fade toward the forward forest. This is an artistic environment inspired by Carezza, not a geographically exact reconstruction.

The lake is opaque geometry with a stereo-aware URP shader, slow analytic ripples and an approximate reflection sampled from the same distant texture. It does not render a second camera or perform screen-space reflection. The nearest shoreline is about 10 m behind the entrance, outside the existing stick-walking limit. The scenery never moves a tracked head or controller.

Spruce foliage uses three fixed intersecting textured planes per tree, with genuine alpha gaps; this is an economical approximation, not fully modeled individual branches. The nearby original scene's trunk geometry is preserved in the single-tree experience. Garden clones receive fresh foliage renderers because statically batched source renderers cannot safely replace their shared mesh while retaining old batch offsets.

Generated combined meshes and runtime material copies are owned by the perimeter component and destroyed when a model replaces the garden. The new environment has no colliders or interaction canvases. Shared artwork is serialized on `ForestGardenView`; `CarezzaEnvironmentInstaller.Apply()` imports textures and updates the scene through Unity Editor APIs. Scene cameras use a far plane of 400 m to include the 220 m ridge from the whole garden.

## Artwork and generation

All three runtime textures were created with the built-in ImageGen tool, then copied into `Assets/VRExperienceAGB/Art/Carezza/`. Unity generated and retains their `.meta` files. The generated asset outputs, not the reference concept board, are used in the scene. Android import uses ASTC 6x6 and mipmaps; the maximum import sizes are 4096 for the panorama, 2048 for spruce and 1024 for ground. Actual source dimensions can be smaller than the requested generation dimensions.

Prompt set:

- **LatemarPanorama.png:** One continuous transparent panoramic mountain/forest strip based on the selected Moonlit Carezza reference. Central irregular vertically fissured Latemar cliffs, lower wooded ridges on the sides, opaque blue-green spruce forest along the base. No sky, moon, stars, text, panels, water, reflections, foreground objects or near ground. The follow-up edit preserves silhouette, geometry, forest, composition and alpha, changing only rock to pale chalky ivory and warm milk/cream limestone, slight dusty rose/peach on lit facets and beige mineral bands, with desaturated lavender-blue shadows. Restrained night/blue-hour appearance; no orange sunset, glow or bright white snow.
- **AlpineSpruce.png:** A single complete mature alpine spruce, front orthographic elevation on genuine transparent alpha. Irregular asymmetric staggered drooping branch fans, transparent gaps, visible muted brown trunk, dark blue-green needles and sparse frost on upward surfaces. Cool moonlight, readable interiors, no solid cone, thick snow, floor, shadows, halo, text or extra trees. Intended for fixed crossed foliage planes.
- **AlpineGround.png:** Seamless square top-down albedo of alpine lakeshore soil, fine pale limestone grit, tiny cream/gray gravel, low flattened green-brown grass and moss, sparse frost. No large rocks, protruding plants, objects, horizon, directional shadows, text or borders. Neutral muted gray-brown base with green variation and beige grains; cool tint is applied in the shader.

Internet references used during concept selection: [Latemar from Lake Carezza](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Massiccio_del_Latemar_dal_lago_di_Carezza.jpg), [Alpe di Siusi from Seceda](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alpe_di_Siusi_-_View_from_Seceda.jpg), and [Alps Field HDRI](https://polyhaven.com/a/alps_field). ## Side atmosphere and interior firs follow-up

The user approved the rear mountains on Quest and requested softer/smaller side ridges plus a few of the earlier decorative firs among the model trees. The side panorama now gradually reduces its visible height to 48% and blends 72% toward a blue atmospheric color between 55 and 100 degrees away from the rear center. The rear central view is unchanged. Scaling happens in texture coordinates on the continuous curved mesh to avoid tapered-panel distortion; the lake reflection uses the same side adjustment. No full-screen or volumetric fog is added.

Six original radial-branch firs are restored between planter columns in the 100-tree layout, using the existing Moonlit branch and trunk meshes and blue-frost foliage. Up to three pairs are distributed along the model, outside the central aisle and before the crosswalks. Short ensembles receive fewer pairs; single-row models receive none. ## Ground contact and open interior crowns follow-up

The user reported floating trees behind the observer and requested larger interior firs with visible trunks and branches, like the one-tree clearing. No new device screenshots were present in the standard Quest capture folders, so inspection used the latest saved scene captures plus fresh Editor views.

The spruce texture's alpha-tested extent is x=124..923 and y=29..1507 within 1024x1536 pixels. Previously the transparent lower margin lifted the visible trunk by about 1.8% of each tree's height. Card UVs now exclude that margin and embed the visible base slightly in the ground. Both cloned and scattered scenery trees are placed on the ground; positions inside the lake margin move outside the irregular bank. A live mesh inspection found 72 combined shoreline trees, zero elevated bases and zero bases within the water margin.

The six interior firs are now approximately 7.8–9.4 m tall, with wider crowns. A garden-owned variant of the original seven-layer branch mesh omits the lowest layer and staggered branch cards to expose the trunk and separate boughs. Fourteen woody limbs per fir reuse the one-tree clearing's bark mesh and branch construction, combined into one shared mesh. The original single-tree artwork is unchanged. Generated crown and limb meshes are released with the garden during model replacement.
