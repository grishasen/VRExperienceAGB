WinterStars.csv is an adapted subset: Sun removed; visual magnitude <= 6.0;
columns restricted to HYG ID, proper name, right ascension (hours), declination
(degrees), apparent visual magnitude and B-V color index (missing index = 0.65).
Coordinates retain the catalog's J2000 mean equatorial reference.
